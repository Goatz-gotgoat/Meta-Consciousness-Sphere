The Recursive Continuum folded into a sphere, part of Recursive Process Monism (RPM).

- [Meta-Consciousness Field](meta-consciousness-field.html): the 3D particle field, full screen. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom.

**What the figure shows**

- **Reality** sits at the front of the sphere, where the phenomenological chain (two people, each lived and seen) crosses the material chain (cosmos to atoms).
- **Horizon of Knowing**: the far side, where all four chains meet. It is the limit of human understanding, reached from the lived side and the seen side alike.
- **Meta-consciousness** sits at the core. Every surface node loops into it through a figure-eight, except the quantum limits (field, entanglement, foam), whose lines are pushed outward to the horizon.
- **The dark background** stands for consciousness itself: unlit, not empty. Meta-consciousness illustrates how it interconnects.

Duality holds from within, nonduality holds for the whole, and the gap between them is the Hard Recursive Bind. The quantum labels mark where description runs out; they express a proposed philosophical relationship, not a demonstrated physical mechanism.
