    What the sphere says

    Duality holds from within

    Lived and seen, subject and object, self and world are real distinctions. They are how any part meets the rest.

    Nonduality holds for the whole

    None of those distinctions splits the continuum into separate worlds. They all arise inside one whole.

    The bind stays

    A part cannot step outside the whole to see it entire. Every description made from inside leaves a residue, and it never reaches zero.

    The black background stands for consciousness itself. Dark here means unlit, not empty. Consciousness is what everything appears in, so it is never one of the things that appear. Meta-consciousness at the core is only a way of drawing how it interconnects.

    That explains why the search for consciousness keeps failing in the same way. You cannot find darkness by shining a light on it, because wherever the light lands, the darkness is gone. Observing is a light: it shows things, never the ground they appear in. From the inside the picture flips. There, consciousness is not dark at all. It is the light you see everything by, and a light cannot be lit by another light. Dark from the outside and light from the inside are two descriptions of one thing, which is the figure's duality and nonduality again.

    Quantum physics approaches the continuum from the seen side, through measurement, and has several formulations that never quite close. The study of consciousness approaches it from the lived side, through experience, and stalls at the hard problem. On this view both are parts describing the whole from inside it, so the shared wall is expected. It follows from where the observer stands.

    The quantum labels mark where description runs out. Placing them on the sphere expresses a proposed philosophical relationship. It does not claim a demonstrated physical mechanism.

    Context for later chats

    Consciousness
    The black background, the page included. Every node, loop and horizon appears within it. Dark means unlit from the outside, not empty; from the inside it is the light everything is seen by.
    Meta-consciousness
    The core of the sphere, equally near every point on it. Every surface node loops into it. It is a figure drawn to illustrate how consciousness interconnects; consciousness itself is the dark ground.
    Horizon of Knowing
    The limit of human understanding. The joint behind Reality where the four outer ends meet: as far as any line of inquiry reaches, from the lived side or the seen side.
    The fold
    On the flat map, the Horizon of Knowing is the yellow edge around everything. Folded, that edge becomes one point opposite Reality.
    Outer figure-eights
    Each extremity (lived A, lived B, cosmos, DNA and atoms) runs on through figure-eights to a quantum limit: field at both sides, entanglement at the top, foam at the bottom.
    Core loops
    Figure-eights from every surface node to meta-consciousness at the centre: Reality, both people, both lived selves, cosmos, DNA and atoms, and the Horizon of Knowing. The quantum limits have none. With the core loops the structure is recursive inward and outward, with no privileged start.
    Light and darkness
    Looking for consciousness by observing is like shining a light to find darkness: the light removes what it was sent to find. Darkness is found by its edges and by contrast, which is also how consciousness science makes progress, comparing conscious with unconscious states.
    Hard Recursive Bind
    The permanent gap between the view from within (duality) and the whole (nonduality). It is a consequence of the observer being part of what it describes.
